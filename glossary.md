# Glossary

- **Artifact**: a page published on claude.ai, with a URL such as `https://claude.ai/artifact/<id>`.
- **Mapped artifact**: an artifact with an entry in `artifacts.json`.
- **Source of truth**: the `src` file in this repo. All chats edit this file.
- **src file**: the page content, exactly as published to claude.ai.
- **Pages file**: the `docs/` copy that GitHub Pages serves.
- **Sync hook**: `scripts/artifact-sync.py`, called by Claude Code before and after the Artifact tool.
- **Sankey chart**: the ROI flow chart. Products → departments → Revenue → Gross profit and costs → Net profit, Shared platform and product costs.
- **Gross profit**: Revenue − direct costs (Chat Traffic, Agent runs, Judges, Guardrails).
- **Net profit**: Gross profit − Shared platform (People, Services, Infrastructure).
- **Engagement plan**: the stakeholder map by power × interest. It tells who to engage and how often.
- **Rollout plan**: the stakeholder map by usage × will. It tells where to start AI pilots.
- **Role**: an agent team name in the market research, such as Agent Platform. Each role has 1 classic role, such as Platform Engineering.
- **Grade**: a level in the ladder of a company. IC grades go from Engineer to Distinguished. Manager grades go from Manager to Director+.
- **Role filter**: the role chips in PE2. They hide the role columns. The filter has no effect in the slice Level × Company.
- **Posting**: 1 public job posting of 1 company. PE2 counts 64 postings.
- **Level ladder**: the list of levels of 1 company, from public sources. PE4 shows 7 ladders.
- **Market research**: the data in PE2 to PE5. Source: AI Agentic People Market Research @ Upstars v2.0.0.
- **Summary Sankey**: the chart in the P and L summary cards, in the ROI Sankey style. Profits: products → departments → Revenue. Losses: Losses → departments → products.
- **ROI view**: the L summary card view by product and department, with Shared platform.
- **Audit view**: the L summary card view by the 7 cost categories, through Direct cost and Shared platform.
